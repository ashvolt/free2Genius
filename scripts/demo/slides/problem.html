The modelling decision

Propensity ranks who will convert.
Uplift ranks who converts because you asked.

Sure things
High propensity, zero uplift. Contacting them buys a conversion you already had.
Persuadables
The only group a contact changes. The entire budget should go here.
Sleeping dogs
Negative uplift — contact makes them less likely. Ranking by propensity finds them.
Lost causes
No response either way. Cheap to skip, and skipping them is free budget.
Two-model T-learner plus an X-learner, trained on a synthetic randomised cohort. Evaluated with Qini curves and offline policy evaluation, not accuracy.
